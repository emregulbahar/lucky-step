## Acceptance Criteria

*From `design/game-brief.md`, scoped to this story:*

- [ ] The board is a 3D wooden board game on a table: own Blender models with one painted-wood material, Kenney CC0 cat (painted black) and furniture.
- [ ] Lamp light and a tilt-shift blur background, running in the WebGL build.
- [ ] Lucky steps show a gold glow; the screen edges darken as the counter nears 13 (or a deadly number in World 4).
- [ ] Dice roll, coin flip and flowerpot fall are animated and match the deterministic outcome.
- [ ] Visual direction checkpoint: a short video is shown and approved (brief Build order step 3, Sun 8 Nov).
- [ ] The WebGL build stays within 60 fps on the dev machine's browser.
