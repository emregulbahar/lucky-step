## Own assets

Die, coin, spike, floor tile, gap tile, board rim, wall pieces (playing cards, poker chips, game box), broken mirror
exit portal and cat paw print (`Assets/Art/Models/`) are made for this game by `tools/blender/make_models.py` (Blender 5.1).
Materials in `Assets/Art/Materials/` are generated by `Assets/Editor/BuildBoardScene.cs`.
The UI lock and pawn icons (`Assets/Art/UI/Sprites/icon_lock.png`, `icon_pawn.png`) are drawn by `tools/ui/make_ui_sprites.py`.

Suggested itch.io credit line: "3D character, furniture, bridge and cat models by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), CC0."
